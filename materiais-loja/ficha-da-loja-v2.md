### Full description
```
Espertinhos is a collection of educational games for kids aged 2 to 8 to learn while they play, with a friendly bunny mascot.

Every game has spoken narration, so even children who can't read yet can play on their own. The app is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Portuguese (Portugal): pick the language the first time you open it and change it later whenever you want.

15 GAMES TO LEARN BY PLAYING
🎨 Colors and Shapes — recognize colors and geometric shapes
🔢 Counting — count objects and recognize numbers
🧠 Memory — match pairs of animal cards
🔤 Alphabet — recognize uppercase and lowercase letters
➕ Math — simple addition and subtraction with pictures
🧩 Sequence — find the pattern
🖌️ Coloring — color pictures freely
🔍 Find the Difference — spot the item that is different
⚖️ Bigger or Smaller — compare numbers
🔄 Opposites — find the opposite
🖼️ Puzzle — put the picture back together
🔠 Word Search — find hidden words
🌀 Maze — find the way out
🐶 Animal Sounds — listen and guess which animal it is
👨‍👩‍👧 Family — a special game unlocked by collecting 1000 stars

FOR EVERY AGE
Choose the age group (2-4, 5-6 or 7-8 years) and the games adjust: simpler for little ones, more challenging as your child grows. Every round earns stars, and new levels unlock step by step.

Great for preschool and early learning: alphabet, numbers, colors, shapes, memory, logic and vocabulary.

SAFETY FIRST
• Does not ask for a name, email, photo or any information that identifies your child
• No sign-up and no login
• A "parental gate" (a simple math question) protects settings such as age group, language and voice
• Made for families: no violence, no inappropriate content

Espertinhos is free, with ads suitable for children. If you prefer, you can remove the ads with a one-time purchase inside the app.
```
